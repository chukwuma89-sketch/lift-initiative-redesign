Child Rights & Female Empowerment

To enhance the agency, health, and economic status of women and girls, enabling them to achieve their full potential. This initiative aims to promote child rights, gender equality, reproductive health, and economic empowerment through comprehensive support programs

Implementation approaches

How we deliver

Supporting girls and women to understand their rights, build confidence and reach their potential.

Vocational Skills Training

  • Conduct needs assessments to identify the most relevant vocational skills required in the community.
  • Set up training centers offering courses in areas such as tailoring, hairdressing, computer literacy, and agriculture.
  • Partner with industry experts to provide high-quality training and certification

Reproductive Health Education

  • Provide education on reproductive health and rights through workshops and community outreach.
  • Partner with healthcare providers to offer reproductive health services and resources

Support for Survivors

  • Offer support services for survivors of domestic violence, including counseling and legal aid.
  • Advocate for stronger policies and enforcement against domestic violence.

Mentorship and Role Models

  • Establish mentorship programs connecting young girls with successful women in various fields.
  • Organize motivational talks and networking events to inspire and guide young women.

Educational Support

  • Provide programs to support out-of-school children, and integrate them into the formal education system
  • Implement scholarship programs for girls to return to school and complete their education.
  • Offer after-school programs and tutoring to support girls in their academic pursuits.
  • Raise awareness about the importance of education for girls through community campaigns and workshops.

Economic Support and Entrepreneurial Development

  • Provide micro-grants and low-interest loans to women and girls to start their own businesses.
  • Establish business incubation centers offering mentorship, market access, and business development services.
  • Create platforms for showcasing and selling products made by women entrepreneurs.
