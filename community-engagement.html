Strategic objective 03

Community Engagement

To foster sustainable community development through environmental initiatives, healthcare access, and digital literacy. This initiative aims to enhance the overall well-being and sustainability of communities in oil-producing states and beyond.

Implementation approaches

How we deliver

Mobilising communities to participate in solving the challenges that affect them.

Environmental Sustainability Projects

  • Implement projects focused on pollution control, waste management, and conservation.
  • Promote community involvement in environmental protection activities.

Community Health Initiatives

  • Develop community-based health programs to improve access to healthcare and health education.
  • Partner with health organizations to provide mobile clinics and health camps.

Digital Literacy and Technology Access

  • Establish digital literacy programs to equip communities with essential technology skills.
  • Provide access to computers and the internet to bridge the digital divide.

Youth Engagement Programs

  • Set up youth centers offering recreational and educational activities to engage young people positively.
  • Develop sports, arts, and cultural programs to harness the talents of the youth and channel their energy productively.
  • Organize leadership and personal development workshops to build confidence and skills among youth.
  • Offer leadership training programs and workshops to develop youth leadership skills.
  • Encourage youth participation in community service and civic activities.
  • Promote STEM education through workshops, competitions, and innovation hubs.
  • Partner with educational institutions and tech companies to provide resources and mentorship.
  • Create platforms for youth to voice their opinions and participate in decision-making processes.
  • Engage with policymakers to ensure youth perspectives are considered in policy development.
