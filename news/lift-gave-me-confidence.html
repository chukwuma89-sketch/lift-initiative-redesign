LIFT Gave Me Confidence

Impact Stories · 15 October 2026

I am dark, I really like when I see women doing well in the community, I am a Yoruba, I love eating, I can be kind hearted and set boundaries at same time, I love when I look good and smell nice.

What’s Your Favourite Thing to do?

Doing video of myself, smiling, eating, typing and designing, sleeping.

How has LIFT impacted your journey?

It has given me confidence.

– Adenle Adetoke Favour, LIFT Participant
