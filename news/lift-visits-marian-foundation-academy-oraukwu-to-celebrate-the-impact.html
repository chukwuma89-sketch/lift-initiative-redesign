LIFT Visits Marian Foundation Academy, Oraukwu to Celebrate the Impact of Education-Focused Community Investment

Programmes & Events · 27 June 2026

At LIFT, we believe that meaningful change begins with creating environments where children can learn, thrive, and aspire to greater possibilities. This commitment was at the heart of our recent visit to Marian Foundation Academy, Oraukwu, where we had the privilege of witnessing the impact of an education support initiative designed to strengthen both the learning environment and academic excellence.

The visit provided an opportunity to reconnect with the school community and see firsthand how thoughtful interventions can contribute to a more inspiring educational experience. From the transformed school environment to the excitement shared by students and teachers, every conversation reinforced the importance of investing in schools in ways that create lasting value.

One of the highlights of the initiative was the recognition of outstanding academic performance through scholarships awarded to the best-performing student in each class. By celebrating excellence, we hope to encourage a culture of hard work, discipline, and continuous learning among students while demonstrating that dedication is worthy of recognition.

Beyond academic support, improvements made to sections of the school’s physical environment have helped create a more engaging and welcoming space for learning. A well-maintained school environment does more than improve aesthetics, it fosters pride, belonging, and motivation among students and educators alike.

During our visit, we were warmly received by the school leadership, staff, and students, whose appreciation served as a reminder that sustainable impact is built through collaboration and shared purpose. Hearing their stories and witnessing the positive changes within the school community reaffirmed our belief that strategic investments in education can influence lives far beyond the classroom.

At LIFT, we remain committed to creating opportunities that empower young people, strengthen communities, and contribute to a future where every child has access to an environment that supports learning, growth, and excellence.

As we continue this journey, we look forward to partnering with more schools and communities to deliver initiatives that leave a lasting impact one student, one school, and one community at a time.
