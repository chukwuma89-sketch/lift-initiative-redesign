Strategic objective 01

Economic Empowerment

To foster the economic empowerment of vulnerable populations beginning with  securing the release of individuals detained in hospitals due to unpaid medical bills. This initiative will ensure their dignity and freedom by providing sustainable economic opportunities and supporting their access to health insurance. The goal is to reduce financial barriers to healthcare and promote economic self-sufficiency among vulnerable populations.

Implementation approaches

How we deliver

Equipping people with skills, knowledge and opportunities for economic independence.

Identification and Assessment

  • Collaborate with hospitals to identify patients detained due to unpaid bills.
  • Conduct assessments to understand the financial and social circumstances of these individuals.

Funding and Release

  • Establish a dedicated fund to pay off the hospital bills of detainees.
  • Partner with hospitals and local authorities to facilitate the immediate release of these individuals upon bill payment.

Economic Strengthening and Income-Generating Activities

  • Develop a comprehensive support system linking released detainees to vocational training programs.
  • Provide seed funding, tools, and resources for small-scale business startups.
  • Organize job placement programs in collaboration with local businesses and industries.

Health Insurance Support

  • Facilitate access to affordable health insurance schemes for released detainees to prevent future financial constraints due to medical bills.
  • Partner with insurance companies to create tailored health insurance plans for low-income individuals.

Monitoring and Follow-up

  • Implement a robust monitoring system to track the progress of released detainees in their economic activities.
  • Provide ongoing mentorship and support to ensure sustainability and long-term success.
